About the system

What this is, and why it exists

Right now, registering a vehicle, renewing a licence, paying tax, and booking a fitness test all live in separate queues and separate systems. This brings them under one NID-linked account, so citizens and officers work from the same live record.

11

Services in one place

Registration, licensing, tax, fitness, transfers, and verification — reached from a single account.

2

Two portals, one record

Citizens track and pay; officers review and approve — both working from the same live application data.

24/7

Always trackable

Every application can be followed stop-by-stop, any time, with or without signing in.

How it works

Three steps, start to finish

1

Sign in with your NID

One verified account links to your vehicles, licence, and application history — no re-entering the same details.

2

Apply and pay online

Submit the service you need and pay through the integrated gateway — no in-person queue to start.

3

Track until it's done

Follow every stage in real time, get notified on updates, and receive your certificate digitally.
